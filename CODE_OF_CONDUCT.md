# Code of Conduct

QuaranGate contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration respectful, evidence-driven, and safe.

Be constructive when reviewing code or decisions. Critique the implementation, evidence, or reasoning rather than the person. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private information, or deliberately disrupt project collaboration.

Security reports require extra care: do not pressure reporters to disclose exploit details publicly, and do not publish private vulnerability information without coordination.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, spam, off-topic, unsafe, or privacy-violating contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its community.

Questions about moderation should be raised privately with the repository owner through an established private channel.
